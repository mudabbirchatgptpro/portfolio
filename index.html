Approach

Three commitments, every project

Backward from evidence

I start from what acceptable evidence of learning looks like, then design the experience that produces it. Every case study below has a measured result because measurement was designed in, not bolted on.

Access is structural

Differentiation and Universal Design for Learning are part of the first draft. If multilingual learners, emerging readers, or students with IEPs need a retrofit, the design was wrong.

Data closes the loop

I instrument what I build, from MTSS dashboards to Bayesian mastery models, so revision is driven by what learners actually did rather than what the designer hoped.

An adaptive tutoring engine, end to end

SituationMost "adaptive" practice software reshuffles a fixed question bank. It does not know when a student has actually mastered a skill, and it goes stale when the bank runs out.

What I designedA tutoring engine where mastery, not completion, is the unit of progress: a Bayesian Knowledge Tracing model estimates each skill's mastery from every response; a 162-skill knowledge graph across nine domains, with verified prerequisite edges, decides what comes next; interleaved retrieval practice keeps old skills warm; and an item generator writes and verifies new questions the moment a skill's bank is exhausted.

EvidenceA working system, and a self-contained slice of it running in your browser right now, no login, in the live demo below.
